Public information for some of my projects. This page covers Bright Steps and Draft The Draft.

## Bright Steps

Bright Steps is a learning app for children from kindergarten through 6th grade. Kids choose a grade and practice math, reading, science, and social studies, with picture-and-voice activities for kindergarten and saved progress so they can continue where they left off.

For more information or to contact us with questions please email us at App_Submissions@hotmail.com
