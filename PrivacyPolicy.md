# Privacy Policy

This policy covers Bright Steps and Draft The Draft.

This policy describes how Bright Steps handles information. Bright Steps is a learning app for children from kindergarten through 6th grade.

We do not ask for a name, email address, or account. We do not collect personal information from children, and we do not sell information or show ads.

## Information stored on the device

The app saves learning progress on the device. That includes questions seen, answers, accuracy, and streaks, plus whether spoken prompts are muted. This information stays on the device and is not sent to us.

Spoken prompts are played on the device. We do not record a child's voice.

The optional web demo stores the same kind of progress in the browser on that device.

## Draft The Draft

This policy describes how Draft The Draft handles information. Draft The Draft is a mock first-round board for the 2027 NFL Draft.

We do not ask for an email address or an account. We do not sell information or show ads.

## Information stored on the device

Drafts are saved on the device. That includes the players picked, when each pick was saved, and an optional nickname for a draft. This information stays on the device and is not sent to us.

On the web board, drafts stay in the browser on that device. On iPhone and iPad, they stay in the app on that device.

A finished board can be shared as a link. The link contains the picks and the date the board was last saved, and it leaves the device only when you copy or share it.
